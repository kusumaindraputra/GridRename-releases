Questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. GridRename works entirely on your computer. The only thing it sends over the internet is your license key, to check it with our payment provider Gumroad, plus a check for new versions.

How do I get Pro after buying?

You receive a license key by email right after purchase. In GridRename, click Upgrade to Pro, paste the key and click Activate.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes, one license works on up to 3 of your own computers. Getting a new computer? Email us and we'll reset your activations.

Does Pro work offline?

Yes. After activation, GridRename checks the license about once a week and keeps working for 30 days without internet.

Can I undo a rename?

Yes, the last batch can be undone with one click. GridRename also never overwrites an existing file.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). macOS and Linux versions are planned.

What if it isn't right for me?

Email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. See the refund policy.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by Gumroad, which accepts credit and debit cards and PayPal.
